Give complete supported step-by-step guidance on the website, with conditional branches and evidence. No dedicated PDF product or Print button. Do not block the browser's native print/save behavior. Encourage return visits by usefulness, not by withholding answers, unnecessary pagination or frustrating navigation. Do not require the owner to repeat these decisions.

Accuracy and evidence remain required. No promise of payment, universal remedies, guaranteed rankings, AdSense approval or revenue. Never invent credentials or conceal AI assistance. Preserve all 71 research dossiers and unrelated records. Continue authorized preview work autonomously; credentials, legally binding terms, purchases, external messages and live production merge require the relevant actual authorization. No autonomous background schedule is implied by this plan.

## First-release scope

A narrow collection of original claim-investigation guides and a categorical evidence checklist. The current three guides cover response identification, supported correction versus review, and deadline-evidence organization. They are editorial investigation aids, not payer instructions or legal determinations. Their full answers remain available on one page with section navigation and sources/limits visible.

Do not market the first release as a complete code lookup or payer adjudication service. The business name can remain DenialCodePro, but homepage/search copy must describe what is actually available. Code-specific pages and the retained research corpus remain held pending evidence and use-rights clearance. Main/live currently differs from this candidate; review its legacy exposure separately, without silently deleting history.

## What we can prepare without buying a code-set license

| Material | Work permitted in this plan | Release condition |
|---|---|---|
| Independently authored investigation recommendations | Write decision steps, record-comparison questions and clearly fictional examples | Original expression; no copied protected source structure/tables; scope and consequential claims reviewed |
| Original UI and tools | Search published guides; categorical checklist; section links | Existing software dependencies' license terms still apply; avoid proprietary code tables and patient uploads |
| Links to official authorities | Link in context with truthful labels, no endorsement implication | Verify destination; linking is not permission to copy its contents |
| Government rules or instructions | Prepare accurate scoped explanations from inspected authority | Confirm provenance and any third-party copyright notices; a government host does not make every embedded item freely reusable |
| Official descriptions, code-list databases, transaction guides, CPT/UB-04/NCPDP materials | Do not incorporate into this initial public candidate | Establish applicable permission/license or another defensible legal basis first |
| Code-number-indexed explanations derived from held sources | Keep held | Own wording alone is not clearance; do not relabel proprietary derivatives as independent writing |

This is a scope strategy to avoid reliance on paid code-set content, not a legal opinion that the entire website requires no licenses or permissions. No new license agreement was accepted. Public availability is not commercial redistribution permission. No paid license or blanket legal clearance is assumed.

## Content acceptance checklist

- Reader can find meaning/context, first check and conditional action without a long introduction.
- Facts, hypotheses, editorial recommendations and mandatory requirements are distinguishable.
- Unknown facts have a useful route; neither automatic appeal nor patient billing is inferred.
- Complete supported answer is on the page; related links offer a genuinely different next task.
- Critical limitations remain expanded; no evidence or exception removed for brevity.
- Consequential statements have precise applicable source support, or remain qualified/held.
- No unsupported numbers, EDI locators, frequency claims or guaranteed results.
- Mobile usability is measured in a capable browser/device environment, not assumed from CSS.

## SEO and monetization

Use descriptive task-oriented headings, readable URLs, stable canonicals, crawlable links and an accurate sitemap. Keep search results noindex and preview noindex. Enable indexing only for the approved live content. No fake review schema, dates, author profiles, bought links or mass-generated keyword pages. Search Console evidence should guide subsequent priorities; no keyword-volume or ranking study has been completed.

Ads must not obstruct steps or mimic actions. Do not optimize time spent by slowing resolution. Measure task usefulness, relevant search success, return visits and page performance alongside real revenue metrics after permission/consent and account setup. No ads on unfinished or navigation-only content. No blanket privacy-compliance claim; confirm actual services and traffic obligations before ad activation.
